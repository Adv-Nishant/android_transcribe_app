Offline Voice Input turns your speech into text entirely on your device. Whether you're writing messages, taking notes or searching, your voice never leaves your phone: the app has no internet permission, no account and no tracking.

VOICE TYPING IN ANY APP
Tap the microphone on a compatible keyboard (such as AnySoftKeyboard or Microsoft SwiftKey) or a website's voice-search button, and your speech is typed straight into the text field. The app registers as your device's speech-to-text provider, so it works with a wide range of keyboards and apps. Keyboards whose mic key switches to a voice keyboard (HeliBoard, FlorisBoard, OpenBoard, …) open the included voice keyboard instead.

LIVE SUBTITLES
Real-time, on-device captions for any audio or video playing on your phone: videos, podcasts, meetings and more.

TRANSCRIBE AUDIO FILES
Share an audio file to Offline Voice Input (or open it with the app) to turn the recording into text.

SPEECH MODELS
F-Droid builds everything from source, so this version comes without a speech model. On first start, the app walks you through adding one in two steps: your browser downloads the recommended model (NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, 485 MB, 25 European languages), then you import the file. You can also import any other transcribe.cpp GGUF model, for example Whisper for 99 languages or a small English-only Parakeet. The app itself never goes online.

FEATURES
• 100% offline: works in airplane mode, nothing is ever uploaded
• Private by design: audio is processed on-device and never stored or sent
• Optional auto-stop after silence and pausing of playing media while you speak
• Translation to English with Whisper models
• Material You design with dark mode
• Free and open source (MIT License)

Languages of the recommended model: Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, Ukrainian.

Note: some keyboards (such as Gboard) only use their own voice typing and can't pass speech to other apps. With those, use the included voice keyboard.

Speech recognition runs on transcribe.cpp (ggml), wrapped in a Rust core with a native Android interface.
